NARRATION BEATS:

The narration is spoken as these numbered beats:
{numbered}

- Set `beat` on every region to the number of the beat whose sentence talks
  about it. The region is drawn exactly while that sentence is spoken.
- An icon and its own caption form ONE region: one box enclosing both.
- The heading at the top belongs to beat 1 and draws first.
- An arrow belongs to the beat of the element it points TO, unless that
  element is already drawn, in which case it belongs to the beat of the
  element it comes from.
- Every beat should get at least one region when its element is visible.
- reveal_order must follow beat order.
