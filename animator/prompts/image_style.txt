Style: a clean whiteboard explainer frame on a pure white background.
Every element is a small, simple hand-drawn doodle icon with an even
black marker outline and flat solid color fills — no gradients, no
shading, no glow, no texture, no 3D, no photorealism. All text is black,
hand-lettered, uppercase marker lettering. Each caption sits directly
below or beside its own icon and appears exactly once. Every icon is
small (about one sixth of the canvas width) and clearly separated from
every other icon, caption, and arrow by generous white space: nothing
touches, overlaps, or sits on top of anything else. Arrows are thin
black hand-drawn lines that stop short of the icons they connect.
Spread the elements evenly across the whole 16:9 canvas. Draw ONLY the
listed elements and text — no extra labels, no decorations, no border,
no watermark.
